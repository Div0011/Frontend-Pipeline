# Pattern Analysis: L'Oréal Groupe Mediaroom — Corporate / Institutional

## Genre: Institutional — Chapter-Based Scrollytelling (Genre 3)

## Core Architecture
- **Stack:** Next.js + custom scroll system
- **Structure:** Chapter-based with atmospheric photography
- **Approach:** Trust and heritage building through restrained motion

## Key Mechanical Patterns

### 1. Chapter-Based Narrative
- 3-4 clearly marked chapters with numbered navigation
- Each chapter = full viewport section
- Chapter transitions marked by environmental change (lighting tint shift, background image swap)
- Progress bar shows chapter position (1/4, 2/4, etc.)

### 2. Atmospheric Grading
- Consistent warm/cool color grading across all imagery
- Every photo and video has same post-processing look
- Typography in same color family as dominant image tone

### 3. Restrained Typography
- Display serif for headlines (Playfair Display or similar)
- Sans-serif for body (clean, geometric)
- Very large type scale: headlines at 6-8rem, body at 1rem

### 4. Media Treatment
- Full-bleed background images with subtle slow zoom (ken burns, 5% over 10s)
- Video backgrounds with no controls, muted, looped
- Images use loading="lazy" with blur-up placeholder

## Why It Works
- Pacing feels established and trustworthy (doesn't rush)
- Consistent grading makes diverse content feel unified
- Chapter structure makes information digestible

## Extraction For Template 3
- Chapter-based navigation with progress indicator
- Consistent atmospheric color grading
- Ken burns effect on hero images
- Large serif typography with generous scale
- Restrained motion (no decorative animations, only purposeful reveals)
