# Reference Client — SmashGuys (the prototype for a "good prospect")

Use this to teach the lead agent what a *good prospect* looks like. We are NOT
looking for "any burger restaurant" — we are looking for businesses with
characteristics SIMILAR TO THIS PROSPECT.

---

## WHY SMASHGUYS WAS SELECTED
- Independent / founder-led burger brand (NOT a franchise or chain).
- Strong existing product identity and loyal local following.
- Established physical presence (multiple locations, real reviews).
- Website was functional but UNDERSELLING the brand:
  - outdated visual design
  - weak mobile UX
  - generic typography
  - no visual storytelling
  - poor local SEO
  - no clear online-ordering CTA

## THE REDESIGN OPPORTUNITY
SmashGuys had high *brand potential* but low *website quality* → a 9+/10
redesign opportunity. The commercial fit was strong because the food/brand was
already good and only the web presence was holding growth back.

## WHAT WE LOOK FOR IN NEW PROSPECTS
Businesses that match this SHAPE, not necessarily this industry:
1. Independent (single- or few-location), founder-led.
2. Real local presence (reviews, ratings, foot traffic).
3. A genuinely good product/brand that the website fails to communicate.
4. Weak on: visual design, mobile UX, local SEO, online-ordering CTA, storytelling.
5. NOT already beautifully designed (no upside), NOT a chain (can't sell into it).

> Rule of thumb: find the "SmashGuys-shaped" business — great food, weak website,
> independent, local. That is a lead worth approaching.
