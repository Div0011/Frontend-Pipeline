# Pattern Analysis: Canals Amsterdam — Corporate / Institutional

## Genre: Atmospheric Institutional — Genre 3

## Signature Motion
- Cinematic pacing with slow, drifting parallax
- Text appears at deliberate moments, not on every scroll tick
- Ambient sound design complements visual pacing

## Mechanical Patterns

### 1. Slow Parallax Depth Ratios
- Depth ratios between 0.02-0.05 (much subtler than the usual 0.3+)
- Foreground elements move at 0.05, background at 0.02
- Creates micro-depth without distracting from content

### 2. Deliberate Text Reveals
- Text doesn't animate on every section — only at narrative beats
- Reveal types: fade + translateY (30px), never scale or rotation
- Timing: text appears after background has fully settled, then waits 1s

### 3. Ambient Backgrounds
- Atmospheric video loops with subtle motion (water, clouds, cityscape)
- Video opacity: 0.3-0.5 behind text (not competing)
- Gradients overlays on video for readability

### 4. Section Rhythm
- Section 1: Full video + title (slow fade in)
- Section 2: Image + text split (50/50)
- Section 3: Full-bleed image with overlaid stats
- Section 4: CTA on clean background

## Why It Works
- Everything feels unhurried — perfect for institutional trust branding
- Depth ratios are so subtle users don't notice them, but feel the spatial quality
- Text placement at narrative beats makes reading feel earned, not automatic

## Extraction For Template 3
- Subtle parallax ratios (0.02-0.05 range)
- Deliberate text reveal timing (not every section)
- Atmospheric video backgrounds with opacity control
- Section rhythm that varies layout (not repetitive)
- Ambient gradient overlays for readability
