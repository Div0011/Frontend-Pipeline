# Pattern Analysis: Resn (resn.co.nz) — Creative Agency

## Genre: Creative Agency — Playful 3D (Genre 4)

## Signature Motion
- Playful, interactive 3D concept work
- 3D scenes that respond to scroll AND cursor with equal priority
- Bold color usage (not dark-mode-default)

## Mechanical Patterns

### 1. Interactive 3D Scenes
- Each project section has its own R3F scene with unique interaction model
- Scene 1: Tilt-shift miniature that follows cursor
- Scene 2: Particle system that responds to scroll velocity
- Scene 3: Object that deforms on hover pressure
- No two scenes behave the same way

### 2. Bold Color System
- Not dark-mode-only — Resn uses bright, saturated backgrounds
- Color blocking: full-viewport sections each with distinct bold color
- Text inverting based on background luminance
- Accents used as functional elements (links, CTAs, dividers)

### 3. Kinetic Typography Hover
- Letter-level hover effects on project titles
- Hovering a letter rotates or scales it independently
- Non-hovered letters dim slightly (opacity 0.5)
- Creates word-level reading engagement

### 4. Scroll Velocity Effects
- Particle speed, rotation speed, and blur tied to scroll velocity
- Fast scroll = dramatic effect, slow scroll = subtle
- GSAP ScrollTrigger `onUpdate` captures velocity

## Why It Works
- Variety in scene interaction prevents portfolio fatigue
- Bold colors are memorable and shareable
- Hover effects reward exploration
- Velocity-linked effects make scrolling feel impactful

## Extraction For Template 4
- Per-section unique 3D interaction models
- Bold color blocking (not dark-mode-only)
- Kinetic typography (letter-level hover)
- Scroll velocity-driven effects
- Interactive scene variety (not same interaction repeated)
