# Brand DNA

KINO is a platform that provides an interactive digital cinema experience

**Products:** interactive films, virtual cinema experiences
**Competitors:** Instagram, TikTok, YouTube

**Audience:** film enthusiasts and creators

**Positioning:** A platform that combines interactive storytelling, immersive experiences, and community building

**Messaging:**  Revolutionizing the way we experience films

**Voice & Tone:** innovative, creative, and community-driven

**Brand Pillars:**
- innovative storytelling
- immersive experiences
- community building

**Visual Directions:**
- cinematic
- immersive
- minimalist

**Industry Trends:**
- increased demand for interactive content
- growth of online communities
- advancements in digital cinema technology
