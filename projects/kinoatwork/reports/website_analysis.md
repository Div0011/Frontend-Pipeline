# Website Analysis — UI/UX Audit

**Pages crawled:** https://www.instagram.com/kinoatwork/

## UI Audit
The site's UI is standard for an Instagram profile, with a profile picture, bio, and feed of posts. The bio mentions that the company is a branding agency and design studio.

## UX Audit
The site's UX is limited, as it is a single-page profile on a third-party platform. The user can view the company's posts and bio, but there is no clear call to action or way to engage with the company beyond the standard Instagram interactions.

## Weaknesses
- The site has limited content and functionality, as it is a single-page profile on a third-party platform.
- There is no clear call to action or way to engage with the company beyond the standard Instagram interactions.

**Responsiveness:** The site is responsive, as it is an Instagram profile.
