# Motion & Interaction Design

## Scroll Narrative
The homepage reveals itself as the user scrolls, with each beat tied to the creative arc, using Lenis smooth scroll and GSAP ScrollTrigger pins/reveals

## Cursor Interactions
Custom cursor with aura/glow trail and magnetic buttons

## Performance Budget
Hard ceiling of 2s TTI and <2s layout thrash

## Animations
### Hero Animation (gsap/load)
Immersive hero animation with mouse-reactive depth and soft physics

### Scroll Reveal (lenis/scroll)
Smooth scroll reveal of homepage sections

### Button Hover (framer-motion/hover)
Magnetic button hover effect with glow trail
