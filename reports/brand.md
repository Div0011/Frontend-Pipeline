# Brand DNA

Smash Guys is a burger kitchen owned and operated by Popo Ventures, aiming to build a 100 Cr brand from scratch

**Products:** smash burgers, wings, mac and cheese
**Competitors:** Burger King, McDonald's, First Watch

**Audience:** foodies and young adults in Bangalore

**Positioning:** high-end burger kitchen with a unique dining experience

**Messaging:** Smash Guys: serving the best smash burgers in Bangalore

**Voice & Tone:** friendly, approachable, and passionate about food

**Brand Pillars:**
- high-quality burgers
- unique dining experience
- community-driven

**Visual Directions:**
- cinematic food presentation
- industrial-chic decor
- vibrant color scheme

**Industry Trends:**
- increasing demand for high-quality food
- growing popularity of burger joints
