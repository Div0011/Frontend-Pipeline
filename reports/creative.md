# Creative Direction

## Art Direction
Smash Guys is a cinematic food brand that evokes the feeling of a high-end burger kitchen, with an emotional tone that is both sophisticated and approachable. The single big idea is to create an immersive experience that transports customers into a world of premium burgers and unique dining experiences.

## Storytelling
The homepage scroll is a narrative arc that takes the customer on a journey from introduction to action, with a hook that grabs their attention, tension that builds anticipation, reveal that showcases the premium quality of the burgers, proof that demonstrates the unique dining experience, and action that encourages the customer to take the next step.

## Typography Direction
A bespoke pairing of a characterful display face (serif or grotesk) and a precise text face, with a contrast that creates a sense of luxury and sophistication. The display face is used for headings and titles, while the text face is used for body text and descriptions.

## Layout Philosophy
Editorial asymmetry with generous negative space and intentional breakouts from the grid, creating a sense of sophistication and premium quality.

## Visual Hierarchy
The eye meets the hero image first, followed by the navigation menu, and then the call-to-action button. The visual hierarchy is designed to create a sense of flow and guide the customer through the website.

## Interaction Philosophy
Motion makes the user feel like they are interacting with a living object, with custom cursor effects, magnetic moments, and scroll-linked reveals that create a sense of wonder and curiosity.

## Design Principles
- Editorial asymmetry
- Generous negative space
- Intentional breakouts from the grid
- Custom cursor effects
- Magnetic moments
- Scroll-linked reveals

## Moodboards
### Cinematic Storytelling
Inspired by Canals Amsterdam, this moodboard creates an immersive experience that transports customers into a world of premium burgers and unique dining experiences.

### Warm Humanism
Inspired by SixB Dentaire, this moodboard creates a warm and inviting atmosphere that makes customers feel like they are part of a community.

### Japanese Minimalism
Inspired by Hadaka, this moodboard creates a sense of raw creative authenticity and simplicity that highlights the premium quality of the burgers.
