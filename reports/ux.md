## Information Architecture
The homepage content is grouped into sections: Hero, Introduction, Menu, Unique Dining Experience, Testimonials, and Call-to-Action

## Homepage Flow
The step-by-step scroll narrative is: Hook (Introduction to Smash Guys), Tension (Showcasing the premium quality of the burgers), Reveal (Highlighting the unique dining experience), Proof (Customer testimonials and reviews), Action (Encouraging customers to book a table or explore the menu)

## CTA Hierarchy
Primary CTA: Book a Table, Secondary CTA: Explore Menu, Tertiary CTA: Learn More About Us

## Conversion Strategy
The deliberate path to the business goal is to create an immersive experience that transports customers into a world of premium burgers and unique dining experiences, with a clear call-to-action to encourage customers to take the next step.
