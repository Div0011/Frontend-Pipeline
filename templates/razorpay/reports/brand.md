# Brand DNA

Razorpay is India's first full-stack financial solutions company, providing payment, banking, payroll, and credit tools for businesses through a single fintech platform.

**Products:** Payment Gateway, RazorpayX, Razorpay Capital, Payroll, Thirdwatch
**Competitors:** PayPal, Paytm, PayU, Stripe, Cashfree

**Audience:** Indian businesses, startups, and SMEs

**Positioning:** Razorpay is a disruptor in the digital payments space, committed to building the central nervous system of digital India.

**Messaging:** Power your finance, grow your business

**Voice & Tone:** Friendly, approachable, and professional

**Brand Pillars:**
- Innovative financial solutions
- Customer-centric approach
- Reliable and secure payment processing

**Visual Directions:**
- Modern and sleek design
- Vibrant color scheme
- Simple and intuitive interface

**Industry Trends:**
- Digital payments
- Fintech
- E-commerce
