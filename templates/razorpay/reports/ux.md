## Information Architecture
The homepage content is grouped into sections, including Hero, Introduction, Products, Benefits, Testimonials, and Call to Action.

## Homepage Flow
The homepage flow is designed as a narrative arc, beginning with a hook that introduces Razorpay's innovative financial solutions, building tension through the showcase of its products and services, revealing the benefits and proof of its customer-centric approach, and culminating in a clear call to action that empowers businesses to grow.

## CTA Hierarchy
Primary CTA: 'Power Your Finance, Grow Your Business', Secondary CTA: 'Learn More', Tertiary CTA: 'Get Started'

## Conversion Strategy
The conversion strategy is to guide the user through a narrative arc, showcasing Razorpay's innovative financial solutions, products, and services, and culminating in a clear call to action that empowers businesses to grow.
