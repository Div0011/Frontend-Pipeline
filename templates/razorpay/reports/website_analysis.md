## UI Audit
The site's UI is clean and modern, with a clear and consistent design language. However, some sections could benefit from more whitespace and clearer typography.

## UX Audit
The site's UX is generally good, with a clear and easy-to-use navigation and a focus on showcasing the company's products and services. However, some sections could benefit from more interactive elements and clearer calls-to-action.

## Weaknesses
- Some sections could benefit from more whitespace and clearer typography.
- Some sections could benefit from more interactive elements and clearer calls-to-action.
- The site could benefit from more prominent display of customer testimonials and trust badges.
